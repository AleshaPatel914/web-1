Special Chicken Biryani Recipe

Prepare delicious and aromatic chicken biryani at home with tender chicken pieces, basmati rice, fresh herbs and traditional Indian spices.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500 g chicken pieces
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 cup plain curd
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons biryani masala
  • 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 2 green chillies
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • Whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cloves and cardamom)
  • Salt as required
  • Water as required

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Wash the basmati rice thoroughly and soak it in water for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Marinate the chicken with curd, ginger-garlic paste, biryani masala, chilli powder, turmeric, garam masala, lemon juice and salt. Keep it aside for 30 minutes.
  3. Heat oil or ghee in a large pan. Add whole spices and sliced onions. Fry until the onions turn golden brown.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes and green chillies. Cook until the tomatoes become soft.
  5. Add the marinated chicken and cook for 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Add fresh coriander and mint leaves. Cook until the chicken is partially tender and the gravy becomes thick.
  7. In another pot, boil water with salt and whole spices. Add the soaked rice and cook until it is about 70% done. Drain the water.
  8. Spread the partially cooked rice evenly over the chicken gravy.
  9. Add fried onions, coriander, mint leaves and a little ghee on top.
  10. Cover the pot tightly and cook on low heat for 20–25 minutes until the chicken and rice are completely cooked.
  11. Gently mix the biryani and serve hot with raita or salad.
